# Roadmap

## Available in 0.1.0-alpha.1

- Pinned MineColonies compatibility fixes and separately installed Arcology Horizons pack.
- Building repairs and new Stable, Gatehouse, quarry and plantation modules.
- Public release source, GPL notices, installation instructions and reproducible public JAR build.

## Next priorities

1. Gather alpha feedback and fix reproducible compatibility issues; validate multiplayer separately.
2. Connect the internal protected-supply design to native hut inventories with safe, persistent handoffs and player controls.
3. Expose multi-building outpost founding using existing MineColonies huts, workers and requests.
4. Add scouting and Mayor-assisted outpost planning/development.

## Later design goals

- Cross-biome resource automation and configurable transport.
- Colonist ambitions, unique appearances and recovery/rescue systems.
- Building specialization, progression and possible colonist evolution.
- Ars and other magic-mod integrations after the base addon is ready.

These are plans, not advertised functionality. There are no release dates or promises of compatibility with all modpacks.
