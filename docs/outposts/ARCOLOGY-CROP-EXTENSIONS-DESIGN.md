# Arcology crop extension design: ten missing categories

Source audit and candidate layouts, 2026-10-11. Targets MineColonies 1.1.1403-1.21.1 and the local NeoForge 21.1.250 Minecraft sources. This is a design only: no generator, blueprint, native behavior, or existing Plantation asset is changed. Sugar cane, cactus, and bamboo retain their integrated legacy layouts and acceptance coverage. No Ars integration or new worker profession is proposed.

## Registration is a separate field contract

These ten crops require **Plantation Field extensions**, not extra tags added to the Plantation hut. `BuildingPlantation.updateFields()` deliberately registers only the legacy sugar/cactus/bamboo types. A new field uses block `minecolonies:blockhutplantationfield` (`facing=north`, `mirror=false` initially) and tile id `minecolonies:plantationfield`, not `minecolonies:colonybuilding` or an invented worker hut.

Each separate candidate contains one field anchor and its `blueprintDataProvider` with relative corners, schematic identity and `posTagMap`. Put exactly one category's field tag at relative `(0,0,0)` and its crop tags at the appropriate relative locations below. `TileEntityPlantationField.getPlantationFieldTypes()` actually scans all tags in the map, but anchor tagging is the deliberate unambiguous convention. Its `getWorkingPositions` adds the tile's world position to matching relative crop coordinates. Do not store absolute world coordinates or fabricate the extension's persisted `workingPositions` list in a blueprint.

`BlockPlantationField.setPlacedBy()` creates each matching registered extension, passes the crop positions through the module's `getValidWorkingPositions`, then registers a nonempty result with the colony building manager. Complete geometry must exist when this validation runs, especially for cocoa. `PlantationField.isValidPlacement()` permits the field block or the legacy Plantation hut; that compatibility does not make the ten new types part of the hut's legacy registration path.

Assignment is separate from discovery: `BuildingExtensionsModule.assignExtension()` associates the registered extension with a Plantation only if capacity and research checks pass. `PlantationFieldsModule` permits `ceil(level/2)` distinct crop modules, i.e. 1/1/2/2/3 at hut levels 1–5. The large-plantation research adds one extension slot but does **not** raise that distinct-crop count. Existing legacy fields count toward owned slots/types. Tests must use separate eligible Plantations or explicitly reassign fields; never bypass the native limit to attach all ten to one hut.

Field construction uses `WorkOrderPlantationField`, whose build target level is one. These are ten one-stage pieces, not ten five-level building families. Proposed paths are `infrastructure/plantationfields/<category>1.blueprint`, with category names from the first column below; this path convention is a design choice. Preserve native typed NBT, include complete pack/path identity through normal placement, and do not seed colony IDs, ownership, research, or worker state into the asset. The field tile also persists top-level name/path/pack and a byte rotation/mirror value; derive these from a verified native serialized field when implementing rather than copying ordinary-hut metadata. Native rotation/mirror and reload tests are mandatory.

## What each tag means, and what can grow there

| Category | Tag semantics and native operation | Environment and output obligations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cocoa | Tag **jungle-log cells**. `TreeSidePlantModule.getValidWorkingPositions()` expands each tag to horizontally adjacent air and shuffles/caps the result at 20. Those derived air cells become planting/harvest positions. Cocoa harvest requires age >=2. | Use actual `minecraft:jungle_log`: the module's facing selection tests this exact block, although vanilla survival accepts the broader jungle-logs tag. Leave adjacent cells empty during initial registration. Mature cocoa must not be prefilled in those derived cells. Native random growth needs no light threshold in the inspected block implementation. Supply cocoa beans and an axe through the hut; require age progression, bean consumption and harvested beans. |
| Vine | Tag **every intended vine cell**, not the supporting wall. The module keeps `ceil(20% * workingPositions)` planted, planting separated cells when below that number and harvesting excess. Default maximum 20 positions. | Give each cell a valid sturdy side/overhead attachment. Native `getPlantingBlockState` computes faces from neighbors. Natural spread needs `doVinesSpread=true` and loaded surroundings. No light threshold appears in the inspected growth logic. Supply vines and shears; count natural expansion beyond retained stock and native harvested items. A lone decorative vine is insufficient. |
| Kelp | Tag **substrate cells**. Plant at tag+up; harvest tag+2up once at least two kelp blocks exist, retaining the bottom segment. Selection uses a probabilistic height/25 check; deciding an already selected operation uses the minimum length. Default cap 20. | Sturdy non-magma bottom, two or more cells of actual water above; choose source water throughout. Kelp grows only into water. Vanilla loot needs shears. Short seagrass implements NeoForge `IShearable`; native generic tool selection can request shears with the Plantation settings, despite the module declaring none. Growth/planting distinction matters: the worker uses direct native block placement and inventory reduction for plant actions. The asset should supply substrates/supports/water, not pre-grown output. Vanilla survival must still hold after neighbor updates. Native harvest uses `holdEfficientTool` and `mineBlock`, not solely the module's nominal tool declaration. Provide shears as well as crop-specific items where loot requires them and inspect what the worker actually equips.

## Arcology layouts

Use a **10 × 6 × 10** shell for each category: two by two five-block modules, perimeter frame, familiar oak/spruce first-stage palette, restrained stone/water basin accents, y=5 roof plane. These single-stage field pieces need no artificial L2–L5 sequence. Keep functional logs, nylium and coral visible inside that common frame. Use full blocks for walking surfaces, roof lights outside crop volumes, and no doors or gates across the three-wide front entry x=4..6. Lighting supports visibility; it does not replace missing water/substrate or invent a crop light requirement.

### Dry floor family

Floor y=0, worker feet y=1, anchor `(7,1,1)`, north-facing with clear interaction route; corners relative to it `(-7,-1,-1)` and `(2,4,8)`. Keep front z=1..2 and central access x=4..5 clear. Where a specified crop area overlaps this access, use the perimeter path instead and verify a connected access map; do not move tags casually to make the path test pass.

- **Cocoa:** two jungle logs `(3,1,5)` and `(6,1,5)`, both tagged `cocoa`. Their eight horizontal neighboring air cells are the expected initial derived positions. Keep the entire surrounding 3×3 rings clear at y=1..2; no preplanted cocoa. The logs can have untagged upper decorative continuation only where it does not block those access cells.
- **Vine:** a support wall x=2, z=3..7, y=1..3; tag the ten air cells x=3, z=3..7, y=1..2. Native retained target is two vines. Access strip x=4..5 remains clear. Untagged decorative vines are omitted so their drops cannot be mistaken for production.
- **Glowberry/weepingvine:** four support blocks `(3,4,4)`, `(6,4,4)`, `(3,4,7)`, `(6,4,7)`, tagged with the category's crop tag. Reserve each vertical column y=1..3 as air; y=0 stays solid. Keep adjacent x=2/4/5/7 routes unobstructed. Plant and harvest action positions are below the support, so test those positions rather than trying to path onto the roof.
- **Twistingvine:** four tagged full substrate blocks `(3,0,4)`, `(6,0,4)`, `(3,0,7)`, `(6,0,7)`; reserve their columns y=1..4. Perimeter and central paths remain available as stems grow.
- **Crimson/warped:** a nine-cell nylium patch x=3..5, z=4..6 at y=0, each tagged. This intentionally uses part of the nominal central access; reachable perimeter lanes x=2 and x=6 and front/rear cross-lanes provide working access. Keep y=1..4 over the patch free of placed decorations. Surrounding non-nylium floor limits off-plot vegetation; inspect spillover rather than assuming bone meal honors blueprint tags.

### Raised pond family

For kelp, seagrass and seapickle use two **2 × 3** basins: x=2..3 and x=6..7, z=4..6, bottom y=0, source water y=1..2, air y=3..4. Solid dry rim/dock surfaces at y=2 surround the basins, including central x=4..5; worker feet there are y=3. The dock connects through the front using a three-wide graded approach: entrance floor y=1 at z=0 and dock y=2 from z=1. The interior north strip is filled to dock height so returning workers cannot drop into a trench. Anchor `(7,1,0)` on the north wall; relative corners `(-7,-1,0)` and `(2,4,9)`. The basin bottom rests on exterior ground instead of requiring a deep excavation.

For **kelp**, tag six bottom cells x=2 and x=6, z=4..6, leaving the neighboring water cells as growth/collection space. For **seagrass**, tag all twelve bottom cells. For **sea pickles**, use live coral throughout the twelve-cell bottom but tag only eight cells: x=2,3,6,7 at z=4 and z=6. This respects the cap of ten while leaving a central spread row. Bone meal may grow untagged pickles there; native collection and leftovers must be measured.

The raised dock preserves two water growth cells inside the six-block shell. The field tile is on the dry north wall, not submerged. Physical containment uses full basin walls; decorative waterlogged stairs cannot supply uncontrolled side flows. Static tests must require fluid amount 8 at each crop water cell after settling, rather than merely the presence of a water identifier in a serialized palette.

## Navigation and remaining risks

`EntityAIWorkPlanter.workField()` passes the **action position**, not the original crop tag, to `getPositionToWalkTo`. Most vine modules select a horizontal air neighbor; kelp searches upward for air; bone-mealed fields use the action position directly. `AbstractEntityAIBasic.walkToSafePos()` delegates with safe-destination mode and a distance of four, checked after path completion. This makes nearby dry paths plausible for overhead or underwater work, but does not establish their runtime accessibility. Record the derived working position, action position, requested walking target, native path result and actual citizen position in each test.

Specific runtime risks remain: underwater seagrass's target is the bottom substrate; kelp's target is air over water; overhead planting may select elevated air without support; cocoa registration happens before mature crop occupancy; hanging and aquatic drops may land away from the worker; glowberry growth/loot is probabilistic; tall seagrass and nether byproducts may need a different tool than short seagrass. These are explicit native acceptance gates, not reasons to bypass AI or inject output. A source-supported layout is not yet a proven production farm.

## Native acceptance plan

1. **Metadata/build discovery:** build each field through the native field work-order/placement path in an isolated colony. Verify real field tile, matching extension registry/module, transformed world tags, nonempty valid working positions and native assignment. For cocoa verify exactly the expected derived air positions at initial registration. Check native builder completion ordering, rotation/mirror and save/reload; do not manually write runtime working-position NBT to hide failed registration.
2. **Research/capacity:** verify locked categories reject assignment without their effect, and accept with an explicitly disclosed established-colony research fixture. Sea pickle's no-effect behavior follows the pin. Use one category per suitable worker run, retaining separate legacy-three regression coverage and unchanged original assets.
3. **Production:** start with empty crop spaces and only substrates/supports/water plus disclosed seed/fertilizer/tool inventory in the hut. Observe native seed/fertilizer consumption, living planted blocks, natural growth or actual native bonemealing, a harvest transition, and net collected crop output. Latch transient growth before harvest. Count by item identity and distinguish planted stock from newly produced output. Report generic native action/experience evidence where available, without inventing crop-specific statistics.
4. **Per-category proof:** cocoa age two; vine population exceeding the two retained starters; kelp length two and surviving base; seagrass generated underwater plus equipped shears and item drops; pickle waterlogged four-count group; glowberry actual berry-bearing growth and collected berries; each Nether vine retained base/head and real drops; nylium fields generated vegetation plus fertilizer consumption and collected representative fungus/roots. Use bounded but generous native tick windows for probabilistic growth, no post-start growth-age/AI mutations or inserted harvested items.
5. **Access and continuity:** all runs preserve worker identity, show movement from the hut to the extension, disable recovery teleports, and finish without persistent path failure or drowning. Record water settling and nylium survival over time. After a harvest verify a second productive cycle where practical; a one-off seeded harvest must not be described as natural production.
